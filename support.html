Support

Something not working, or a question the guide doesn't answer? I'm happy to help.

Email

Email ribbonappsupport@gmail.com

Ribbon is made by one person, so replies can take a few days.

To help me help you

  • What happened, and what you expected
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (iPhone Settings → General → About)
  • Ribbon's version (Ribbon → Settings → About)
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Crop out anything private.

Please don't send your backup file or private reminder details unless I ask and you're comfortable with it. I'll delete anything you send once your question is sorted.

Quick fixes

  • No notifications? See Why didn't my reminder go off?
  • Subscription alerts not breaking through Focus? Turn on Time Sensitive Notifications in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Ribbon.
  • New iPhone? See How do I move to a new iPhone?
