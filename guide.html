How to use Ribbon

A quick tour of the basics. Ribbon is meant to be gentle, so there's nothing you have to set up beyond your first reminder.

Getting started

When you first open Ribbon, Miso (the cat) says hello and asks for your first name. This is optional, and only used for your greeting. Ribbon then asks to send notifications. Allow them, or reminders can't alert you. You can change this later in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Ribbon.

Adding a reminder

Tap the + button at the bottom right.

  1. Type what you want to be reminded about.
  2. Under When, pick a date and time, or tap one of the quick choices.
  3. Optionally choose a Repeat, Star it, add Tags or write Notes. Notes support simple formatting like bold and lists.
  4. Tap Save.

To change a reminder later, tap it, then tap Edit.

Typing it in one line

You can type the whole thing in the title, and Miso picks out the details. A Picked up card shows what was understood. Tap Use now to fill it in, or just Save. If you change your mind after Use now, tap Undo.

  • Vet tomorrow 9am #miso
  • Remind me at 6pm to put the bins out becomes "Put the bins out" at 18:00
  • Gym every Monday and Thursday 7am
  • Water plants every other Tuesday
  • Call mum this weekend, Dentist next week, Pay rent end of the month, Pick up parcel after work

If Miso doesn't pick anything up, your words are left exactly as you typed them.

Repeats and time zones

Reminders can repeat every day, every weekday, every week, month or year, or every few of those (for example, every 2 weeks). Weekly reminders can repeat on chosen days. Tap the days in the M T W T F S S row.

If you travel, a reminder normally follows your iPhone's time zone ("8:00 wherever I am"). You can also pin a reminder to a particular time zone, for things like a call at a fixed time abroad. Clock changes for daylight saving don't move your reminders.

Home

  • Starred reminders sit at the top.
  • Coming up shows what's next.
  • Whenever you're ready lists anything that's slipped past. It's marked "To catch up", with no guilt.
  • A weekly recap appears at the start of each week, and recent activity is at the bottom.
  • Tap Miso to say hello.

Done, snooze and Fresh start

  • Done: tap the circle on a reminder, or swipe right. A repeating reminder moves on to its next time.
  • Snooze: swipe left, or tap a reminder and choose Snooze. From a notification, press and hold it for Done, Snooze and Snooze 1 hour.
  • Star or Delete: swipe, or press and hold a reminder. Deleting can be undone straight away from the message at the bottom.
  • Fresh start: on Home, next to "Whenever you're ready". It moves everything you haven't caught up on to its next time (one-offs go to tomorrow) without marking anything done. You can undo it for a few seconds.

Finished one-off reminders are archived after 30 days, which you can change in Settings. Archived reminders are hidden from lists but still show up in search.

Tags and shortcuts

Add tags in the Tags field, or type #something in the title. If you make a typo, Ribbon suggests the tag you probably meant. Tap a tag to see everything with it, change its colour or rename it. Renaming a tag to the name of another one merges the two.

The bar at the bottom of the screen has room for three pinned tags, so you can jump straight to them. Pin and arrange them in Settings → Bottom bar shortcuts. Press and hold a shortcut to add a reminder with that tag already chosen. A tag's page has a button for this too.

Subscriptions

Ribbon is laid-back on purpose, but subscriptions cost money if you forget them. Tag a reminder #subscriptions (or #subscription) and it becomes firmer:

  • a warning 3 days and 1 day before;
  • a Time Sensitive alert on the day, which can break through Focus;
  • follow-ups 2, 4 and 6 hours later if it isn't marked Done;
  • it alerts even if you've turned off Sound or Reminder alerts in Ribbon's Settings;
  • snoozes are limited to an hour at a time, and Fresh start leaves it alone.

When a reminder has the tag, the editor shows a Subscription card where you can tap Add a cost if you want to. The billing period follows the reminder's Repeat (monthly if it doesn't repeat). The #subscriptions tag page shows how many you have and a rough monthly and yearly total.

To let these alerts through Focus, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Ribbon → Time Sensitive Notifications is on. To turn the firmer alerts off for a reminder, remove the tag.

Finding things

Use the search button on Home to search all reminders, including archived ones. Filter by date (Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, Past), status (Upcoming, To catch up, Snoozed, Completed) or tag.

Lock Screen countdowns

When a reminder is coming up soon, Ribbon can show a live countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with Done and Snooze buttons. Choose how early countdowns start, or turn them off, in Settings → Lock Screen & Dynamic Island. You can also start one from a reminder's details. Countdowns start when Ribbon is open, and not when it's closed.

Streaks and Miso

Repeating reminders build a streak (shown with a small flame) when you do them in a row. Miso forgives one missed time now and then, so a single slip doesn't reset everything.

Streaks earn Miso accessories: a bell at 3, a bow tie at 7, yarn at 14, a top hat at 30 and a crown at 100. Miso's red ribbon is there from the start. Choose what Miso wears in Settings → Miso's wardrobe. Miso also dresses up for the seasons, which you can turn off.

Prefer less cat? Turn off "Show Miso's messages on Home", or turn on "Calm Miso" so Miso stays relaxed whatever's waiting.

Siri and Shortcuts

Say "Add to Ribbon" or "What's next in Ribbon", or use them in the Shortcuts app. Adding understands the same one-line typing as the app. Both ask for Face ID or your passcode first.

Lock and privacy

Ribbon can ask for Face ID (or your passcode) every time it opens. Turn this on or off in Settings → Privacy & security. Ribbon's content is also hidden in the app switcher.

Notifications show the reminder's title on your Lock Screen. Notes (and subscription prices) stay hidden unless you turn on "Show notes in notifications". Everything stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Backups

Ribbon keeps its own copies on your iPhone, one a day for the last 7 days. To keep a copy elsewhere, or move to a new iPhone, go to Settings → Backup → Save a backup to Files. To bring it back, use Restore from a backup. Ribbon asks before replacing anything. The saved file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
