Frequently asked questions

The basics

Is Ribbon free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. Ribbon works completely offline and makes no network requests of its own. There's nothing to sign up for.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 27 or later. There isn't an iPad, Mac or Apple Watch version, and Ribbon doesn't sync between devices.

Does Ribbon use my Apple Reminders or Calendar?

No. Ribbon keeps its own reminders and doesn't read or change Apple's Reminders, Calendar or anything else on your iPhone.

Who's Miso?

Ribbon's cat. Miso keeps you company, reacts to your progress and earns accessories from your streaks. If you'd like less cat, turn off "Show Miso's messages on Home" in Settings, or turn on "Calm Miso".

Notifications

Why didn't my reminder go off?

Check these, in order:

  1. iPhone Settings → Notifications → Ribbon: Allow Notifications is on.
  2. Ribbon → Settings → Notifications: Reminder alerts is on. Use "Send a test notification" to check.
  3. A Focus (like Do Not Disturb or Sleep) may be holding it back. Add Ribbon to the Focus's allowed apps, or tag it #subscriptions if it's a subscription.
  4. Your iPhone's date, time and time zone are correct (Settings → General → Date & Time).
  5. Open Ribbon now and then. iPhone only lets an app schedule a limited number of notifications ahead, and opening Ribbon tops them up.

Ribbon can't guarantee every reminder arrives, so don't rely on it alone for anything critical, like medication. See the Terms of Use.

Why is Miso asking me to open Ribbon?

iPhone limits how many notifications an app can line up in advance. If you have lots of repeating reminders and haven't opened Ribbon for a while, the list runs short. The evening before that happens, Miso sends one quiet note. Open Ribbon for a second and the list refills.

Can I hide what my reminders say on the Lock Screen?

Notes and tags are hidden from notifications and countdowns unless you turn on "Show notes in notifications". To hide titles too, go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Ribbon → Show Previews → When Unlocked (or Never).

How do I change the sound or the snooze length?

Ribbon → Settings → Notifications: choose an alert sound (or your iPhone's default) and how long Snooze lasts.

What happens when I travel or the clocks change?

Reminders keep the time you picked. By default they follow your iPhone's time zone (9:00 stays 9:00 wherever you are). Pin a reminder to a time zone in the editor if it needs to happen at a fixed time somewhere else.

Reminders

What does "To catch up" mean?

Reminders whose time has passed without being ticked off. Ribbon doesn't nag about them. They wait under "Whenever you're ready" on Home.

What does Fresh start do?

It moves everything you haven't caught up on to its next time: repeating reminders to their next occurrence, one-offs to tomorrow at the same time. Nothing is marked done, and you can undo it straight away. Skipping past a repeating reminder this way can end its streak. Subscription reminders stay where they are.

How do streaks work?

Doing a repeating reminder each time in a row builds its streak. Miso forgives one missed time, and that forgiveness comes back after 7 more in a row. Streaks earn Miso accessories at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100.

Where did my finished reminder go?

Finished one-off reminders are archived after 30 days (you can change this in Settings). They're hidden from lists but still turn up in search, and under Archived in All reminders.

I deleted something by mistake.

Tap Undo in the message that appears straight away. After that, it can only come back by restoring a backup you saved to Files earlier, which replaces everything in Ribbon with that backup.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say "Add to Ribbon" or "What's next in Ribbon", or use them in the Shortcuts app. They ask for Face ID or your passcode first.

Subscriptions

What does the #subscriptions tag do?

It makes that reminder firmer: warnings 3 days and 1 day before (fewer for reminders that repeat every few days, so warnings don't overlap the previous one), a Time Sensitive alert on the day, and follow-ups 2, 4 and 6 hours later until it's marked Done. It alerts even if Sound or Reminder alerts is off in Ribbon, and snoozes are limited to an hour at a time. See the guide.

How do I turn the firmer alerts off?

Remove the #subscriptions tag from the reminder. There's no setting that turns it off for every reminder. The tag is the switch.

My subscription alert didn't break through Focus.

Go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Ribbon and turn on Time Sensitive Notifications. Also check that the Focus you're using allows time-sensitive notifications (Settings → Focus → choose the Focus → Apps).

Does Ribbon connect to my bank or cancel subscriptions?

No. Ribbon only knows what you type in. It never connects to your bank, cards or the App Store, and it can't cancel or change anything. Cancelling is up to you, with the provider.

Are the monthly and yearly totals exact?

No, they're estimates from the costs you've entered. Each cost follows the reminder's Repeat: weekly prices are multiplied by 52 (times the number of chosen days), monthly by 12, and the monthly figure is the yearly total divided by 12. Costs on reminders that don't repeat, and costs in a different currency from your iPhone's, aren't counted.

Privacy and your data

Can you see my reminders?

No. Your reminders are kept in a file on your iPhone, encrypted by iOS when your iPhone has a passcode. Ribbon never sends them to me or anyone else. Read the Privacy Policy.

Can I lock Ribbon?

Yes. Settings → Privacy & security → "Require Face ID to open". It falls back to your iPhone passcode.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

If you move using an iCloud backup, a computer backup or Quick Start, Ribbon's data comes with it. Otherwise, use Settings → Backup → Save a backup to Files on the old iPhone, then Restore from a backup on the new one.

Why isn't the backup file encrypted?

So you can restore it on any iPhone without Ribbon needing an account or password. Inside Ribbon your data is encrypted by iOS. Once you save a backup to Files, it's an ordinary file, so keep it somewhere private.

What happens if I delete Ribbon?

All its data is deleted from your iPhone, including its daily copies. Save a backup to Files first if you want to keep anything.

Help

Is Ribbon accessible?

Ribbon supports VoiceOver, larger text sizes, and light and dark mode. If anything is hard to use, please let me know.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Email ribbonappsupport@gmail.com. Your iPhone model, iOS version and Ribbon version (Settings → About) help. If you're a beta tester, you can also send feedback through TestFlight.
