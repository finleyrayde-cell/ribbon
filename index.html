Ribbon

Gentle reminders, kept on your iPhone.

Like a ribbon round your finger. Ribbon reminds you about the little things without nagging, with Miso the cat for company. No account, no ads, no tracking, and nothing ever leaves your iPhone.

Just type it

"Gym every Monday and Thursday 7am #health" sets the time, the repeat and the tag in one go.

Kind, not naggy

Missed something? It waits under "Whenever you're ready". Fresh start moves it all on, with no guilt.

Firm about money

Tag a reminder #subscriptions for warnings 3 days and 1 day before, and alerts that break through Focus.

Private by design

Stored only on your iPhone, encrypted by iOS, with an optional Face ID lock.

Streaks with grace

One missed day is forgiven. Streaks earn Miso a bell, a bow tie, a top hat and more.

Lock Screen countdowns

See what's next on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, with Done and Snooze buttons.
